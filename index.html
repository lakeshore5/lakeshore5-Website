About My Work

I'm a professional Minecraft builder with more than a decade of experience across the game's most demanding projects - custom build commissions, hand-sculpted terrain and worldbuilding, and studio-scale productions for Minecraft Marketplace and NFT-adjacent environments. My work covers fantasy cities, classical and baroque palaces, science-fiction environments, underwater worlds, large-scale organic and character sculpture, and full Marketplace-grade Minecraft maps.

I trained on Qubion in the early 2010s, joined Athion as staff and a build reviewer in 2017, and continue to review builds and mentor other creators there today. I co-own Xenos Builds, a professional Minecraft build team responsible for projects like Christmas 2231, CMA World, FutureX, and Dragon Point Castle, and I co-own Mythos Creations (mythosbuilds.com), a studio delivering Marketplace and studio-scale Minecraft productions. Through those studios and my own work, I've collaborated with Razzleberries, Ayres, Nitric Concepts, and independent builders across the industry.

Every project on this portfolio was designed and built with intent - terrain, architecture, interiors, and atmosphere composed together rather than assembled from parts. If you need a custom Minecraft build, a full custom world, or a Marketplace map delivered to production standard, that's the work I do. Details on services, background, and how to get in touch.
