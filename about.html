About lakeshore5

Professional Minecraft builder since 2016 - Athion staff, Minecraft Marketplace contributor, and co-owner of Xenos Builds and Mythos Creations.

A Career Built Block by Block

I'm lakeshore5. I've been building in Minecraft since 2012 and working professionally since 2016. What began as a hobby in the game's early years grew - through formal training, staff work, and Marketplace production - into a full-time career focused on large-scale environments, custom terrain, architecture across styles, detailed interiors, and organic sculpture.

My foundation was laid on Qubion in 2015, where I went through structured builder training via their in-house ranking system and learned the fundamentals of palette, composition, silhouette, and detailing that still guide every project. In 2016 I joined Athion (play.athion.net), one of the longest-running professional builder communities in Minecraft, and in 2017 I was promoted to staff - reviewing hundreds of builder applications and portfolio submissions, running live build reviews, and teaching techniques to the next generation of builders.

From there my work moved onto the Minecraft Marketplace, contributing to projects with established partner studios including Razzleberries, Ayres, and Nitric Concepts. In late 2021, my friend creeper5777 and I became co-owners of Xenos Builds, taking over from our friend Optoculium and continuing the studio's professional Marketplace and NFT World work. In 2026 I became co-owner of Mythos Creations (mythosbuilds.com), a studio focused on high-craft commissioned worlds and long-form worldbuilding projects.

My range covers fantasy and medieval architecture, classical and Greco-Roman work, contemporary and futuristic sci-fi, industrial and steampunk, and organic sculpture - cliffs, trees, sea life, and character work. Toolset is professional-grade: WorldEdit, VoxelSniper, Axiom, custom brushes and heightmaps, schematic design, and clean survival-friendly output when the project calls for it.

Alongside client work I share techniques and build breakdowns on YouTube and stream live building sessions on Twitch. If you're looking to commission a build, terrain project, or full custom world, the contact page has everything you need to get started.

Building Philosophy

"Every block placed should have a purpose, every structure should tell a story, and every build should inspire others to create."

My approach combines technical execution with design intent - palette discipline, silhouette reads, layered detail, and interiors that carry the same craft as the exteriors. The goal isn't the biggest build; it's the build that feels most alive to walk through.

Whether the project is a Marketplace map, a full custom world through Mythos Creations, a Xenos Builds team production, or a solo commission, the same standard applies. If you'd like to commission a build, terrain project, or full custom world, get in touch via the contact page.
