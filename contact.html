Frequently Asked Questions

What's a typical project timeline?

Small commissions (single structures) usually run 1–3 weeks. Mid-size projects (palace + grounds, small terrain map) 4–8 weeks. Full worlds and Marketplace productions are 2–6 months depending on team size and scope. Timeline is agreed in writing before work begins.

Do you work solo or with a team?

Both. Solo for smaller commissions and personal-scale projects. Team production runs through Xenos Builds (since 2021) or Mythos Creations (since 2026) - scaled up with additional builders, terrain specialists, and coordinators depending on the brief.

What formats do you deliver?

Standard delivery is a world file plus schematics of major pieces. For Bedrock Marketplace projects, output is packaged to Marketplace specifications. For terrain projects, region files are included when useful. High-resolution renders and documentation on request.

Java or Bedrock?

Both. Most portfolio work is Java Edition (WorldEdit / VoxelSniper / Axiom workflow). Bedrock is fully supported for Marketplace productions, with build conversion handled in-house when needed.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. For studios, publishers, IP holders, and pre-release Marketplace / NFT projects, NDAs are standard practice. Agreements are signed before any production briefing.

How do revisions work?

Reasonable revisions are built into every project schedule at defined milestones (typically blockout, mid-detail, and final). Larger scope changes are quoted as a new pass rather than absorbed silently - everyone stays on the same page.

What styles do you cover?

Fantasy, medieval, classical & Greco-Roman, contemporary, futuristic / sci-fi, industrial & steampunk, and organic sculpture (cliffs, trees, sea life, character work). See the portfolio for range.

What about usage rights?

Custom private commissions typically include full usage rights for the client. Marketplace, NFT World, and studio productions run on team or publisher agreements - rights and revenue share are agreed up front in the contract.

When should I route through Mythos Creations?

For high-craft commissioned worlds, long-form worldbuilding, and cinematic environment work, Mythos Creations (mythosbuilds.com) is usually the right studio. Tick the "Mythos Creations Inquiry" option in the form and I'll route the conversation there.
