Marketplace & studio-scale work

Beyond one-off commissions, I take on studio-scale professional productions - Minecraft Marketplace maps, NFT World environments, and long-form worldbuilding for teams and clients. These projects are delivered either solo or through the two studios I co-own: Xenos Builds (since 2021) and Mythos Creations (since 2026, mythosbuilds.com).

Published and portfolio-visible work in this category includes Marketplace collaborations with Razzleberries, Ayres, and Nitric Concepts; the SkuxxVerse and CMA World projects; the Nitric Palace production with Nitric; and Royal City - a separate Nitric Concepts release available now on the Minecraft Marketplace.

How professional projects run

Scoping. Studio projects start with a written scope covering deliverables, platform, milestones, review points, and rights. For NDA'd work, agreements are signed before any production begins.

Team assembly. Depending on scale and style, projects are staffed solo, through Xenos Builds, or through Mythos Creations. Studio work brings in additional builders, terrain specialists, and coordinators as required.

Production. Milestone reviews, tracked revision windows, and platform-specific packaging (Bedrock Marketplace requirements, world file conventions, schematic libraries).

Delivery. Final packaged output plus source materials as agreed - world files, region files, schematics, and documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work on Minecraft Marketplace maps?

Yes. I've contributed to Marketplace-published work with Razzleberries, Ayres, and Nitric Concepts, and I co-own Mythos Creations, a studio focused on Marketplace and studio-scale productions.

Can you deliver production-ready Marketplace assets?

Yes. Marketplace work is delivered to platform-standard requirements - clean world files, correct chunk boundaries, and appropriate structure for the target skin pack or map.

Do you take on NFT World and studio productions?

Yes. Nitric Palace, SkuxxVerse, CMA World, FutureX, and Xenos Castle are all examples of professional NFT / studio-scale environments in the portfolio.

Can I hire your studio directly instead of you as an individual?

Yes. For larger productions, work goes through Xenos Builds or Mythos Creations, both of which can staff up to production requirements.

What size teams do you assemble for professional projects?

From a solo build to a full studio team, matched to the scope of the project. Studio work through Mythos Creations regularly involves multiple builders, project management, and QA passes.

Do you sign NDAs and work under contract?

Yes. NDAs, IP assignment, and standard commercial contracts are normal for studio and Marketplace work.

Publishing or producing a Minecraft project?

If you're a studio, publisher, or IP holder building for Marketplace or a full custom world, get in touch - I'll route the conversation through the right studio.
