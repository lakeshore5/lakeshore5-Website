Project Overview

Christmas 2231 is a collaborative build-battle project that reimagines holiday traditions in the year 2231. The design blends festive cheer with cutting-edge sci-fi architecture to produce a complete space-age Christmas village, delivered under a serious time constraint without sacrificing thematic consistency.

Sci-Fi Holiday Design

The village combines futuristic structures, floating islands, and innovative reinterpretations of holiday tropes. Palette and detailing are pulled from both sci-fi industrial language and traditional Christmas iconography, sitting side by side in a single readable world.

Fully Finished Interiors

Every building across the village is built out on the inside as well. Decorated homes, festive shops, and themed rooms are all finished, so the entire wonderland is explorable from storefront through to private space.

Speed and Consistency

The whole project was delivered as a two-person build battle in under one week - a good demonstration of rapid, coordinated production that still holds a high standard for detail and thematic control.
