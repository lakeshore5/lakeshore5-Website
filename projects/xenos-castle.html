Project Overview

Xenos Castle was designed as the primary environment for an interactive castle siege minigame. The scope was to build a fortress that read as a real defensible place and worked as a battlefield, then produce it inside a one-day window with a collaborator.

Fortress Design and Combat Flow

The castle is composed around siege gameplay: massive outer walls, layered battlements, tactical positioning for attackers and defenders, and clean sightlines between the outer ring and the central keep. The design balances historical fortress language with the geometry a multiplayer siege actually needs to feel readable.

Fully Built-Out Interiors

The entire castle is finished on the inside. Great halls, barracks, corridors, and chamber-to-chamber interiors run all the way from the outer walls into the keep, so combat and exploration flow continuously through the whole structure rather than hitting empty facades.

Role and Collaboration

Built with creeper5777 under Xenos Builds. Xenos Castle is a good example of how quickly a small, coordinated team can deliver a production-ready environment without sacrificing internal detail.
