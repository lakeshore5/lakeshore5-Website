Project Overview

SkuxxVerse is an expansive commercial Minecraft world built for the NFT Worlds platform. The scope was to design an environment that reads as a coherent, explorable universe rather than a collection of loose set-pieces.

World Composition

The build features multiple biomes, interactive elements, and carefully composed landscapes stitched together into one continuous world. Sightlines and transitions were treated as their own design problem so movement through the environment feels intentional.

Finished Interiors Throughout

Every major structure across the world is fully built out on the interior. Rooms, shops, and interactive spaces run throughout, ensuring the experience holds up as strongly indoors as it does from the outside.

Role and Collaboration

Delivered as an NFT Worlds commission under Xenos Builds, with creeper5777 and Kimbert.
