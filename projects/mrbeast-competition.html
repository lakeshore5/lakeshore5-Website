Project Overview

MrBeast's Countries Build Competition was a two-week, 1,500×1,500 build event featuring an all-star lineup of top global Minecraft builders competing to capture the essence of a country. Our team took on the United States.

My Contributions

My work on the build included 100% of the terraforming (delivered in under two days), several city blocks, the Seattle Space Needle, composition of the six surrounding islands (Northwest, Midwest, Hollywood, Hawaii, East Coast, and the South), the Alamo and its surrounding structures, Uncle Sam, the Washington Memorial and other monumental East-Coast architecture, atmospheric details, placement of organic and tree elements produced by InfinityBuilds, and a large amount of the touch-up, integration, and thematic pass across the whole build.

Team and Role Clarity

InfinityBuilds designed the organic and tree assets; I placed and integrated them into the environment. creeper5777 contributed as a build partner across the wider project. This kind of role clarity matters - the competition was a genuine collaboration among some of the best builders in the game, and it's important that credit reflect that.
