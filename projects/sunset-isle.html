Project Overview

Sunset Isle is a small personal environment focused on mood and atmosphere. The whole build was completed in a single day and exists to demonstrate how much a compact, well-composed landscape can convey with restrained architecture and strong lighting.

Terrain and Landscaping

Beaches, ridges, and pathways were sculpted to guide movement through the island. Dense tropical vegetation and carefully placed ground cover keep every angle busy without becoming cluttered.

Atmosphere

Warm golden-hour lighting and a soft, saturated palette do most of the emotional work. The island reads as a place to slow down inside, which is exactly the point.
