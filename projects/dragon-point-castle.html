Project Overview

Dragon Point Castle is a fantasy fortress built as a four-way collaboration. The design blends traditional medieval fortress architecture with fantastical dragon motifs - the dragon isn't a prop, it's woven into the castle's silhouette and identity.

Fortress and Dragon Integration

The castle uses towering spires, layered stonework, and dramatic sculptural dragon forms as anchor points in the composition. The dragon reads as part of the fortress rather than a decoration on top of it, which keeps the fantasy language and the defensive language on the same page.

Fully Finished Interiors

Every tower, hall, and chamber is built out on the inside. The castle reads as a living fortress rather than a facade - a consistent standard across the whole build so exploration inside holds up as strongly as the exterior.

Village and Grounds

Beyond the castle proper, the project includes a supporting village and terrain that frame the fortress in its landscape.
