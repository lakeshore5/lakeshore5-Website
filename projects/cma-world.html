Project Overview

CMA is a large commercial Minecraft world commissioned through the NFT Worlds pipeline. The scope was to deliver a complete virtual ecosystem: multiple biomes, settlements, and architectural styles integrated into a cohesive design that reads as one continuous environment.

World Design and Biomes

The world moves between grand palaces, bustling villages, quiet lakes, PvP arenas, and player plot districts. Terraforming and architecture were designed together, so transitions between biomes and between styles feel continuous rather than snapped together.

Fully Finished Interiors Across the Map

Every major structure across the map is fully built out on the inside as well as the outside. Palaces, villages, PvP areas, and player plots all have detailed interiors throughout, so no space feels like an empty shell when players explore inside.

Role

Delivered under Xenos Builds in collaboration with creeper5777. CMA is one of the strongest examples in the portfolio of large-scale commercial Minecraft world production.
