Project Overview

FutureX is a fully realized sci-fi city designed as an interactive RPG environment. It combines dense urban planning with advanced architecture: towering skyscrapers, research laboratories, defensive bastions, and connected districts that form a cohesive vision of a future metropolis.

Urban Design and Districts

The city is broken into districts that read distinctly from a distance but connect legibly at ground level. Sightlines, transit paths, and vertical circulation are all planned so players can navigate the city by moving through it rather than needing a mini-map.

Fully Built-Out Interiors

Every skyscraper, lab, and bastion is fully finished on the interior. Rooms, corridors, and functional spaces run through every visible building, giving players a truly complete sci-fi city to explore rather than a hollow skyline.

Role and Collaboration

Built with creeper5777 under Xenos Builds. FutureX demonstrates the ability to design an urban RPG environment at scale while holding a consistent visual and interior standard across every structure.
